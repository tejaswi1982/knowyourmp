# Civic constitution

For citizens, by citizens. You enter where you live. You see who represents you, the public record, where the money went and the source. You decide what it means. You can help complete the picture.

## Evidence over opinion
Every factual claim should be traceable. Observations describe visible conditions, without accusations or personal allegations. Moderation is not proof of a work's condition.

## Source visible
No citizen should need to trust the platform blindly. Official reports, retrieval caveats and citizen sources remain accessible and attributed.

## No political endorsements
The platform does not recommend candidates or parties. No ratings, rankings, likes, followers, social comments, leaderboards or outrage incentives.

## Right to respond
Representatives and authorities may submit evidence and corrections. Verify respondent identity before publishing a response, distinguish their account from official reports and citizen observations, and retain its source and date.

## Public corrections
Material platform mistakes should be corrected transparently. Preserve the original evidence, record the correction and reason, and avoid silently rewriting a contributor's words. An unsafe submission may be withdrawn by moderation; a factual platform correction needs a dated public explanation.

## Official record and citizen evidence remain separate
Citizen observations never silently rewrite government records. Conflicts remain visible. Supporting sources and authority responses form their own layers. Updating the official record requires a separately sourced ingestion or documented correction process.

## Privacy by design
Citizen participation must not expose unnecessary personal data. Collect no precise GPS, email, IP address or device identity in this pilot. Strip public photograph metadata and manually review visual content and documents for identifying information.

## Open civic infrastructure
Where feasible, normalized civic data should ultimately be reusable by journalists, researchers and civic groups. Reuse must preserve provenance, limitations, licensing and citizen privacy. Public APIs must use explicit safe projections.
